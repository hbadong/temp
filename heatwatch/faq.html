Questions players already type

A page earns its URL when three signals line up: the same question repeats, current answers are weak, and one visit can settle one job.

Signals used

  • Search autocomplete keeps completing the same phrase.
  • Official posts and player boards ask it in different words.
  • Existing results are a 3,000-word recap that still hides the answer.
