Why this page ships in the first six

Search prompts repeat “release date” more than any other phrase. Existing results are often a timeline dump. A short table that a player can scan on a phone is enough. Best loadouts and tier lists are not this page’s job.

Google’s public rules do not ban AI-assisted writing. The risk is publishing pages with no added value whose main job is to manipulate rankings. Six honest pages beat two hundred guessed articles.
